From first message to paid order.

  1. Connect your channels

    Scan a QR code for WhatsApp or sign in with Meta for Instagram and Messenger. Nothing to install.

  2. Teach it your business

    Upload PDFs and FAQs, add your website, or sync products from your store. It answers only from what you give it.

  3. Let it sell

    It answers questions, recommends products, quotes your prices and sends payment links, day and night.

    Each conversation is scored for buying intent. Hot leads are flagged, and you can take over any chat in one click.
